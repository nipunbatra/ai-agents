## Answer

Here are the sourced facts regarding AI assistants, agents, and initiatives in India, based on the latest available information up to November 2026:

### 1. Agriculture & Rural AI Assistants
**Digital Green Farmer.Chat**
*   **Date:** Piloted in 2023; scaled up through 2025–2026.
*   **Organisation:** Digital Green (in collaboration with OpenAI, Microsoft, and Gooey.AI).
*   **What it does:** A generative AI-powered virtual agronomist that allows smallholder farmers to ask questions via text, voice, or images on WhatsApp and Telegram. It provides tailored, localized advice on crop management, pest control, and climate resilience.
*   **Languages:** Multilingual, supporting languages like Hindi, Telugu, Odia, Swahili, and Kikuyu (drawing from a database of videos in over 40 languages).
*   **Scale:** Used by approximately 250,000 farmers, with users averaging 18 interactions per month.
*   **URL:** [farmer.chat](https://farmer.chat)

**Microsoft and AI4Bharat Jugalbandi**
*   **Date:** Launched in May 2023.
*   **Organisation:** Microsoft, AI4Bharat (IIT Madras), and OpenNyAI.
*   **What it does:** A generative AI chatbot accessible via WhatsApp designed for rural India. It retrieves information on government assistance programs (usually written in English) and relays it back to users in their local language via voice or text.
*   **Languages:** Covers 10 out of India's 22 official languages.
*   **Scale:** Covers information for 171 government programs.
*   **URL:** Accessed via WhatsApp; backend models at [ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in).

**PM-Kisan Kisan e-Mitra**
*   **Date:** Launched on September 21, 2023 (upgraded with LLMs in February 2024).
*   **Organisation:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare, Govt of India (AI developed by Wadhwani AI, powered by Bhashini).
*   **What it does:** A voice and text AI chatbot integrated into the PM-KISAN portal and app. It handles queries regarding payment status, eligibility, the Kisan Credit Card, and the PMFBY crop insurance scheme.
*   **Languages:** 11 regional languages.
*   **Scale:** Handles over 8,000 farmer queries daily. As of December 2025, it had answered over 93 lakh (9.3 million) queries.
*   **URL:** Integrated into [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in).

### 2. National Language & Sovereign AI Platforms
**Bhashini**
*   **Date:** Ongoing (integrated into major portals like e-Shram in 2025).
*   **Organisation:** Digital India BHASHINI Division under MeitY.
*   **What it does:** A national AI-powered language translation platform providing APIs for speech recognition, machine translation, and document intelligence to break digital language barriers.
*   **Languages:** 22+ official Indian languages.
*   **Scale:** Used across massive government deployments, including the Maha Kumbh 2025 and the e-Shram portal.
*   **URL:** [bhashini.gov.in](https://bhashini.gov.in)

**Sarvam AI (Samvaad & Indic Voice Agents)**
*   **Date:** Company founded in 2023; Samvaad launched publicly in early 2026.
*   **Organisation:** Sarvam AI.
*   **What it does:** Provides "Samvaad", an agentic AI platform allowing enterprises to build, test, and deploy conversational voice agents that can conduct real-time phone calls for customer support, sales, and lead qualification.
*   **Languages:** 11+ Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, and Punjabi.
*   **Scale:** Enterprise-scale deployments (e.g., integrated with MoEngage for outbound AI voice campaigns). Pricing operates at scale starting around ₹3 per minute.
*   **URL:** [sarvam.ai](https://sarvam.ai) / [indus.sarvam.ai/samvaad](https://indus.sarvam.ai/samvaad)

### 3. Government & Healthcare Services
**Government of Gujarat AI Initiatives**
*   **Date:** Ongoing (2025–2026).
*   **Organisation:** Government of Gujarat (Forest Department, GIL, GSWAN).
*   **What it does:** The state utilizes the Gujarat State Wide Area Network (GSWAN) for e-governance. Recently, citizen-facing bots like the **FOREST AI Agent** have been deployed on the state's Forest Department portal to assist users with e-services, licenses, and grievances.
*   **Languages:** English and Gujarati.
*   **URL:** [forests.gujarat.gov.in](https://forests.gujarat.gov.in) / [gswan.gov.in](https://gswan.gov.in)

**eSanjeevani Telemedicine**
*   **Date:** Launched in 2019; hit a major milestone in September 2026.
*   **Organisation:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) and C-DAC Mohali.
*   **What it does:** India's National Telemedicine Service, providing Doctor-to-Doctor (assisted) and Patient-to-Doctor remote consultations.
*   **Scale:** Crossed **50 crore (500 million) teleconsultations** on September 15, 2026. It supports over 1.42 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandir spokes, 19,473 hubs, and has over 2.41 lakh registered healthcare providers.
*   **URL:** [esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in](https://esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in) *(Note: Often accessed via state portals or the eSanjeevani app)*.

### 4. Banking, Insurance & Customer Support
**NPCI (DigiSaathi & UPI Help)**
*   **Date:** DigiSaathi launched March 2022.
*   **Organisation:** National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI).
*   **What it does:** DigiSaathi is an automated AI chatbot/helpline for digital payment queries. NPCI also runs the "UPI Help Assistant" for conversational support regarding UPI transactions and mandates.
*   **Languages:** 10 languages (English, Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Gujarati, Punjabi, Malayalam, Odia, Assamese).
*   **URL:** [digisaathi.info](https://digisaathi.info) / [npci.org.in](https://npci.org.in)

**HDFC Bank (EVA)**
*   **Date:** Launched in March 2017.
*   **Organisation:** HDFC Bank (built by Senseforth AI).
*   **What it does:** EVA (Electronic Virtual Assistant) is an AI-powered banking chatbot handling queries on accounts, cards, loans, and branch services.
*   **Scale:** Historically handled over 20 million interactions per month. In 2025, EVA handled approximately 2.7 million customer queries with an 85% accuracy rate.
*   **URL:** [hdfcbank.com](https://hdfcbank.com)

**Jio (HelloJio & Jio Haptik)**
*   **Organisation:** Reliance Jio / Jio Haptik Technologies.
*   **What it does:** "HelloJio" is an enterprise-grade Voice AI platform for customer engagement and in-car voice assistants. Jio Haptik provides enterprise AI customer service agents.
*   **Scale:** Haptik claims automation rates of 85-90% for enterprise customer service, saving hundreds of thousands of human agent hours. Jio's broader voice infrastructure processes billions of calls.
*   **URL:** [haptik.ai](https://haptik.ai) / [jio.com](https://jio.com)

### 5. Infrastructure: India AI Mission Compute
*   **Date:** Cabinet approved on March 7, 2024.
*   **Organisation:** IndiaAI (Independent Business Division under MeitY).
*   **What it does:** A strategic initiative to democratize AI compute capacity by providing subsidized GPUs to startups, academia, and MSMEs.
*   **Scale:** Initially aimed to deploy 10,000+ GPUs at a highly subsidized rate of ₹65 per hour. By early 2026, over 38,000 GPUs had been onboarded. Recent reports (September 2026) indicate the government is now targeting **100,000 public GPUs** by December 2026 to establish sovereign AI capabilities. Private partners like Yotta are building "Shakti Cloud" powered by over 20,000 NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs to support this ecosystem.
*   **URL:** [indiaai.gov.in](https://indiaai.gov.in)
