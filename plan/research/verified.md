## Notes

- **Pages that blocked the fetcher (HTTP 403):** openai.com, gartner.com, canlii.org, civilresolutionbc.ca, catalyst.nejm.org, pib.gov.in (HTML pages), aim.security and nature.com (login redirect). For these I used search-indexed text from the primary page, marked "VERIFIED (snippet)", or a secondary source, marked SECONDARY. The Wayback Machine could not be reached from this environment.
- **SWE-bench Verified (#16):** Don't present 80.9% as the current record. Two framings work on a slide:
  - "Vendor-reported scores went from 33% (GPT-4o, Aug 2024) to 81% (Opus 4.5, Nov 2025) to 94% (Claude Mythos Preview, Apr 2026). In Feb 2026 OpenAI said the benchmark is contaminated and stopped reporting it."
  - "The independent leaderboard tops out at 79%."
  - Anthropic's Opus 4.7 and Opus 5 pages carry memorization-screen footnotes and give no Verified number in their text.
- **OSWorld (#18):** Agents now exceed the 72.36% human baseline on OSWorld-Verified: 90.2% for an agent framework and 86.0% for a single model, as of Aug 2026. Anthropic's Opus 5 page reports on "OSWorld 2.0" rather than OSWorld-Verified, so a successor benchmark is replacing it.
- **Gemini pricing (#20):** The prices are mid-change. The 3.8 Flash price doubles on 1 Jan 2027, four weeks after the talk. Quote the current price with "rising to $1.50 / $7.50 in January", or name an explicit model instead of the alias.
- **Klarna (#2):** The "hiring humans again" headline overstates it. Per the Bloomberg coverage, the hires are freelance or gig-style remote agents, and overall headcount keeps shrinking.
- **TPMG (#15):** Say "in about 15 months (Oct 2023 to Dec 2024)", or use Permanente's own phrasing of "1,794 working days".
- **Kisan e-Mitra (#13):** Use the PIB Sep 2025 figures: 11 languages, 95 lakh+ queries, 20,000+ a day. Treat later press figures (93 lakh in Dec 2025) with caution.
- **Digital Green (#14):** The counts differ across Digital Green's own pages within weeks. "1.6 to 3 million farmers, 10+ languages, 6 countries" is safe.
- **Replit (#4):** One report says Lemkin later recovered the data with Replit's rollback, even though the agent had said rollback was impossible. That detail is SECONDARY.
