## Answer

Here is the sourced evidence regarding ambient AI scribes, customer-support AI agents, and enterprise AI adoption, based on recent data, studies, and reports from 2024 to 2026. 

### (A) Ambient AI Scribes in Healthcare

**The Permanente Medical Group (TPMG) / Kaiser Rollout**
*   **Scale and Impact:** According to *NEJM Catalyst* analyses published in 2024 and 2025, TPMG executed the largest ambient AI scribe deployment in the literature. Between October 2023 and December 2024, 7,260 physicians used the technology across more than 2.5 million patient encounters. 
*   **Time Saved:** The 2025 follow-up analysis reported that the generative AI scribes saved physicians an estimated 15,791 hours of documentation time in one year—equivalent to 1,794 eight-hour working days. It significantly reduced "pajama time" (after-hours charting) and improved both physician and patient satisfaction.

**Randomized Controlled Studies (2025)**
*   **NEJM AI Trial:** A pragmatic randomized clinical trial published in *NEJM AI* in late 2025 by UCLA researchers evaluated two leading ambient AI scribes (Nabla and Microsoft DAX Copilot) against a usual-care control group. The trial included 238 physicians across 14 specialties.
*   **Documentation Time:** Nabla users experienced a statistically significant 9.5% decrease in time-in-note (saving about 41 seconds per note). DAX Copilot users exhibited no statistically significant reduction in documentation time compared to the control group.
*   **Burnout and Accuracy:** Both tools showed roughly a 7% improvement in validated burnout and task-load scores. However, the study noted that notes occasionally contained clinically significant inaccuracies (e.g., omissions or pronoun errors), requiring physicians to remain vigilant in reviewing AI-generated documentation.

### (B) Customer-Support AI Agents

**Intercom Fin & Sierra**
*   **Intercom Fin:** Intercom's AI agent, Fin, operates on an outcome-based pricing model ($0.99 per successful resolution). As of late 2026, Fin's average resolution rate across its 12,000+ customers is reported to be 76% (up from roughly 25% at launch). 
*   **Sierra:** Founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, Sierra provides AI agents for enterprise customer service and recently reached a $15.8 billion valuation. Sierra also utilizes outcome-based pricing and reports resolution rates above 70% for major clients (e.g., Singtel achieved >70% resolution within 10 weeks of launch).

**Salesforce Agentforce**
*   **Headcount Reduction:** In 2025, Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff confirmed that the company reduced its customer-support headcount from approximately 9,000 to 5,000 employees (a cut of 4,000 roles). Hundreds of these employees were redeployed to other departments like sales and customer success.
*   **Resolution Share:** *Uncertainty Flag:* Salesforce's claimed resolution rates vary by source. A 2026 company update stated Agentforce resolved 63% of customer questions, while Benioff claimed in a 2025 podcast that the AI was resolving up to 85% of customer contacts. 

**Klarna**
*   **The 700 Agents Claim:** In February 2024, Klarna announced its OpenAI-powered assistant handled 2.3 million conversations in its first month (two-thirds of all customer service chats) and reduced resolution time from 11 minutes to under 2 minutes. Klarna claimed the AI did the "equivalent work of 700 full-time agents".
*   **The Walkback:** *Uncertainty Flag:* The "700 agents" figure was a workload estimate, not a direct layoff count. By May 2025, CEO Sebastian Siemiatkowski admitted that cost had been a "too predominant evaluation factor," which resulted in "lower quality" service. Consequently, Klarna reversed course and began hiring human agents again to handle complex cases and restore brand trust.

### (C) Enterprise Adoption

**Gartner Prediction (June 2025)**
*   In June 2025, Gartner predicted that **over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by the end of 2027**. 
*   *Uncertainty Flag:* This is a forecast, not a measured historical failure rate. Gartner noted that these cancellations will not be due to model failures, but rather escalating costs, unclear business value, and inadequate risk controls. Gartner also warned of "agent washing"—vendors rebranding basic chatbots as autonomous agents.

**MIT NANDA 'GenAI Divide' Report (August 2025)**
*   **The 95% Figure:** An August 2025 report by MIT's NANDA initiative found that **95% of enterprise generative AI pilots fail to deliver measurable P&L (profit and loss) returns**. 
*   **Criticisms & Nuance:** The report clarifies that the technology itself isn't failing; rather, the failure stems from a "learning gap" and flawed enterprise integration. While top-down corporate pilots stall because companies avoid redesigning workflows, a "Shadow AI Economy" thrives, with 90% of employees successfully using personal AI tools to boost their own productivity. 

**Anthropic Economic Index (2025)**
*   **Automation vs. Augmentation:** Anthropic's September 2025 Economic Index tracked how users interact with Claude, distinguishing between "augmentation" (collaborating/iterating) and "automation" (delegating tasks). 
*   **Directive Use:** The report found that "directive automation"—where users give the AI a task and it completes it end-to-end with minimal human back-and-forth—rose sharply from 27% to 39% over eight months. For enterprise API customers, the automation rate was vastly higher, hitting 77%, indicating that businesses are actively shifting AI from a collaborative tool to a workflow replacement solution. *(Note: By early 2026, consumer use on Claude.ai saw augmentation slightly overtake automation again, showing fluctuating trust levels).*

**McKinsey State of AI 2025**
*   **Adoption vs. Scaling:** McKinsey's late-2025 report found that while 88% of organizations are using AI, only about a third (38%) have actually scaled it beyond the pilot phase. 
*   **Agents and Workflow:** 62% of respondents reported experimenting with AI agents. The defining characteristic of the 5.5% of companies seeing real financial returns (the "high performers") is that they do not just layer AI over existing processes; they are nearly 3x more likely to fundamentally redesign and re-architect their workflows to accommodate AI.
